## B. Specialist purpose and context

**Governing question:** Is engineered quantum entanglement incremental, breakthrough, or potentially Promethean, and how much should broader potential influence a specific organization's present decision?

**Technology definition:** The controlled generation, distribution and verification of entangled states as an information resource. Quantum entanglement is a physical phenomenon, so the emerging technology is its engineering into usable systems.

**Primary case:** A hypothetical conservative US financial institution considering entanglement-based quantum key distribution between two offices over 18 months. It has no verified quantum hardware team or dedicated link. Compare with a standards-based post-quantum cryptography migration.

**Contrast case:** A hypothetical university quantum-network laboratory with optics expertise and dark-fiber access considering a reversible two-node field research pilot over two years. The same general classification must hold while the local action changes. The hype test repeats the bank case with a demand for immediate deployment based on headlines.

## C. Three-level finding and student judgment

**ET generally:** Provisional **potentially Promethean** classification for engineered entanglement as a reusable resource. There is high confidence it is at least a breakthrough: nonclassical correlations are experimentally verified and field demonstrations distribute entanglement over urban fiber. Networking/QKD and sensing are materially different research domains. Broad, economical and reliable social-scale use remains unproven, so confidence in the higher label is moderate-low and it is explicitly a claim of potential.

**ET for the primary application:** A 50 km entanglement-based QKD field experiment demonstrates feasibility in a specific setting, not this bank's production security, uptime, cost or integration. NIST FIPS 203 provides a standardized alternative for quantum-resistant key establishment. A field study is the highest supported evidence stage for the QKD application; no bank deployment is assumed.

**ET in the primary organization:** The case's missing dark fiber, quantum staff and integration evidence make an immediate production QKD rollout unjustified. The appropriate 18-month direction is to prioritize a measured PQC migration plan and, only if relevant and affordable, a separately gated QKD feasibility study. A university lab with suitable staff and fiber can reasonably run a bounded research pilot without claiming a citywide operational network.

**What would change the conclusion:** Independent cross-domain deployment evidence could strengthen the higher general label. Site-specific link feasibility, audited security design, operating costs, performance and continuity evidence could change the bank recommendation. Reproducible field results and working repeater/network capability could expand the laboratory's pilot scope.
