# Evidence checked for the quantum-entanglement worked example

Checked against the original research or issuing organization on 2026-10-18. The technology here is the *engineered use* of entanglement as a generated, distributed and verified resource. Entanglement as a physical phenomenon is not a recent invention. Both organizations are hypothetical. A field demonstration is not proof that either organization has deployed a system.

| --- | --- | --- |
| Q1 | Hensen et al., *Nature* (2015-10-21), [DOI 10.1038/nature15759](https://www.nature.com/articles/nature15759) | Loophole-free Bell test with electron spins separated by 1.3 km demonstrates a robust nonclassical resource. It does not establish a commercial network. |
| Q2 | Kucera et al., *npj Quantum Information* (2024-09-14), [DOI 10.1038/s41534-024-00886-x](https://www.nature.com/articles/s41534-024-00886-x) | Experimental entanglement distribution and quantum-state teleportation over a 14.4 km urban dark-fiber link. Supports an urban field research stage, not citywide scale or production security. |
| Q3 | *Nature* research (2024), [entanglement of quantum memory nodes in a telecom network](https://www.nature.com/articles/s41586-024-07252-z) | Two memory nodes entangled through 40 km fiber spools and a 35 km deployed Boston-area fiber loop. An enabling step toward repeaters, not a deployed large network. |
| Q4 | *Nature* research (2022-11-23), [distributed quantum sensing with mode-entangled spin-squeezed atomic states](https://www.nature.com/articles/s41586-022-05363-z) | A materially different sensing research domain using entanglement. | Q7 | US NIST, [What Is Quantum Cryptography?](https://www.nist.gov/cybersecurity-and-privacy/what-quantum-cryptography) | Explains equipment imperfections, distance/absorption limits and the need for repeaters. This is an agency explanation, not a measurement of our hypothetical bank. |

## Evidence for and against the highest plausible classification

**For potentially Promethean:** Q1 establishes the physical resource. Q2–Q3 show engineered distribution and networking demonstrations, Q5 an entanglement-based security application, and Q4 a materially different sensing research application. A reusable mechanism with plausible effects on communication, computation and sensing is an inference from this evidence, not an observed societal outcome.

**Against a strong Promethean claim:** These sources mainly demonstrate controlled experiments and specific field links. They do not show large, economically viable, independently secure deployment across sectors. Q7 describes physical and implementation constraints; Q6 shows an alternative approach for the financial security goal. Therefore label the general class *provisionally potentially Promethean* with high confidence in breakthrough capability, lower confidence in broad future impact. The bank should not infer production readiness from the general label.
